## What is Generative AI in your own words?

Generative AI creates new content like texts,images,audios,videos based on patterns it learns from the data.
 
## How did your understanding change from Day 1 to Day 15?

Day 1: I thought it just do things automatically.
Day 15: I understand it works by predicting words or features step by step and uses probability. I also know why models like LLM, GAN, and Diffusion are different.

## one model you found difficult and why?

Diffusion Models were hard because they add noise gradually and remove it to generate data. The process was tricky to visualize.

## One key insight you gained?

Small changes in the prompt or input can make big differences in the output. Prompt design is very important.
