## Capstone problem chosen

Prompt Generation Assistant — AI that creates helpful prompts for text, images, or other AI tools.

## Model selected and why

LLM (Large Language Model) like GPT-4 — because it understands text, generates new prompts, and can adapt to style or tone.

## One challenge faced

Controlling creativity — sometimes the AI gave too generic or too unusual prompts.

## One major learning outcome

I learned how prompt design and sampling control affect AI outputs and how generative AI creates content step by step.

## Internship conclusion (3–4 lines)

This internship helped me understand how generative AI works in practice. I learned different models and their uses. Designing mini-projects improved my problem-solving and creative thinking. Overall, it gave me a clear view of AI generation from theory to hands-on work.
