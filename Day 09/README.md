## What changes when depth increases?

When we add more layers:

The network can understand more complicated patterns.
It may take longer to learn.
The hidden layers make better features than the raw input.

## Why ReLU helps in deep networks.

ReLU helps because it keeps gradients strong, so deep networks can learn faster and better.

## Why these internal representations matter more than raw features.

Hidden layers turn raw features into smarter patterns, making it easier for the network to classify correctly.

## What backpropagation really does

Backpropagation tells the network how to fix its mistakes by updating weights.

## Why deep networks are harder to train

Deep networks are harder to train because gradients can shrink or vanish in early layers.

## What vanishing gradients are

Vanishing gradients are tiny gradients that stop early layers from learning.
