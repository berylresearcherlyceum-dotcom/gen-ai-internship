## Task 4: “Explain to a Junior” Task

ChatGPT is a smart AI that writes answers like a human.
When you ask a question, it first understands your words.
Then it guesses the next word one by one to make a full answer.
It does not copy from anywhere, it creates the answer itself.
It tries to add words that make sense together.
Sometimes it adds new ideas to make it interesting.
You can make it more creative or simple using settings.
It also remembers what you asked before in the conversation.
It keeps predicting words until the answer is complete.
The final answer is human-like and easy to read.
Even the same question can have slightly different answers next time.
